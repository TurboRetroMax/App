Sources & methodology +

Place search: Open-Meteo’s global geocoding API, with location data based on GeoNames. GPS lookup is only requested after you tap Detect and uses BigDataCloud’s free client-side reverse-geocoding endpoint.

Electricity: the latest grid-generation lifecycle intensity with broad country coverage from Ember Yearly Electricity Data, served through the Carbon Data API. If a country has no recent factor, the app uses the world average and labels it.

Other activities: car and flight values use average 2026 UK government conversion factors as a reference; electric-car use is estimated from the selected country’s grid intensity. Flights use average short- and long-haul return distances with aviation effects included. Food uses starter estimates of 1.3, 2.1, or 3.1 tonnes per year by selected pattern.

Privacy: the app stores entered calculator values and cached public grid factors on this device. It does not store your GPS coordinates. A typed location is sent to Open-Meteo for search; live coordinates are sent to BigDataCloud only after you choose GPS detection.
